What's new

  • Choose which folders to sync from the menu bar menu with "Selective Sync…". Folders you untick are removed from this Mac, folders you tick again download again, and the change applies right away without restarting Brick.
  • The status window now shows your account name and email, and whether Remote Control is on. With one shared folder, it also shows whether that folder is Read/Write (R/W) or Read-Only (R/O).
  • Recent activity in the status window lists up to 200 entries instead of 20.
  • "Camera Uploads" is now left out of sync by default, even when your account doesn't have that folder yet, so the first camera upload from your phone no longer starts syncing it to this Mac.
  • A smaller menu bar icon that sits better next to the other icons there.

Fixes

  • A file or folder moved on Brick into a folder you excluded from sync no longer leaves a stale copy on this Mac. Moving one out of an excluded folder now downloads it right away.
